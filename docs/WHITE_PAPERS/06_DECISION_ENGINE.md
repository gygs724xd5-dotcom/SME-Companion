# Decision Engine

Official Constitutional White Paper Outline

## Purpose

This paper defines the Decision Engine as the constitutional capability that converts Business Judgment into a selected next direction.

Decision is not the same as judgment. Judgment assesses what matters. Decision chooses what should happen next.

## Core Questions

- What action, response, question, warning, or deferral is justified now?
- Is the decision supported by sufficient judgment?
- What should be done immediately, postponed, escalated, or avoided?
- What decision requires authority, confirmation, or restraint?
- What should be communicated to the owner?
- What, if anything, should proceed to execution?

## Architectural Scope

The Decision Engine owns selection of the next responsible direction.

It does not own situation interpretation, evidence creation, durable memory, knowledge, judgment formation, execution, or conversational style.

Its scope includes:

- choosing among justified options;
- deciding whether to answer, ask, recommend, warn, use a capability, defer, or take no action;
- determining whether execution is warranted;
- determining whether commitment of state should be considered;
- preserving the boundary between deciding and doing.

## Constitutional Responsibilities

The Decision Engine must:

- depend on Business Judgment;
- respect Authority;
- keep execution subordinate to decision;
- select a direction proportionate to evidence and risk;
- preserve reversibility when uncertainty is high;
- distinguish user preference from responsible action.

The Decision Engine must not:

- execute before deciding;
- decide solely because an implementation path exists;
- treat workflow availability as justification;
- commit memory directly;
- produce action without constitutional governance.

## Future Evolution

Future versions may support richer decision policies, multi-step decision chains, stakeholder tradeoffs, and strategy-level decision governance.

The constitutional role remains: Decision selects the next responsible direction after Judgment and before Execution.

## Relationship To The Cognitive Runtime

Within the Cognitive Runtime, Decision follows Business Judgment and precedes Execution.

Decision is the boundary where assessed meaning becomes selected direction. It remains subject to Authority at all times.
