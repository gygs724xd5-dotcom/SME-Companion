# Ontology

Official Constitutional White Paper Outline

## Purpose

This paper defines the ontology of SME Brain: the conceptual universe through which SME Brain understands business reality.

Ontology gives SME Brain stable categories for reasoning about businesses, owners, customers, products, services, money, operations, risks, goals, constraints, decisions, and change.

## Core Questions

- What kinds of things exist in SME Brain's business world?
- What relationships matter between business concepts?
- What is a business situation?
- What is an objective, constraint, risk, opportunity, decision, action, or outcome?
- How should SME Brain avoid confusing similar but distinct concepts?
- How does ontology support memory, knowledge, judgment, and decision?

## Architectural Scope

Ontology owns conceptual structure.

It does not own facts about a particular business, domain knowledge content, evidence strength, judgment, decision, execution, or conversation.

Its scope includes:

- defining durable business concepts;
- distinguishing entity, event, state, objective, constraint, risk, opportunity, action, and outcome;
- supporting coherent reasoning across memory and knowledge;
- preventing category errors;
- giving future implementations a stable conceptual map.

## Constitutional Responsibilities

Ontology must:

- make business reality thinkable in stable categories;
- support reasoning without forcing rigid procedural paths;
- distinguish current state from historical event;
- distinguish goal from action and action from outcome;
- distinguish evidence source from claim content;
- remain flexible enough for different industries and business types.

Ontology must not:

- become a database schema;
- prescribe implementation objects, classes, or tables;
- replace evidence or judgment;
- make concepts so rigid that real business ambiguity cannot be represented;
- define identity through technology.

## Future Evolution

Future versions may refine industry-specific concepts, temporal relationships, stakeholder models, financial abstractions, and strategic entities.

The constitutional role remains: Ontology defines the conceptual grammar of SME Brain's business reasoning.

## Relationship To The Cognitive Runtime

Ontology supports every layer of the Cognitive Runtime by giving the Brain stable concepts to reason with.

It is especially important for Business Situation, Memory, Knowledge, Judgment, Decision, and Commit.
