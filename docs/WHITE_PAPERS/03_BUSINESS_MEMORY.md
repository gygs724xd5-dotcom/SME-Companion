# Business Memory

Official Constitutional White Paper Outline

## Purpose

This paper defines Business Memory as the constitutional capability for preserving durable business context across time.

Business Memory exists so SME Brain can reason with continuity rather than treating every interaction as isolated.

## Core Questions

- What business context deserves to persist?
- What should remain temporary and uncommitted?
- How should remembered information influence future reasoning?
- When is memory relevant, stale, uncertain, or contradicted?
- What is the difference between remembering and knowing?
- What must be governed before information becomes durable state?

## Architectural Scope

Business Memory owns durable business context.

It does not own truth, judgment, decision, execution, or conversation.

Its scope includes:

- preserving stable business facts, preferences, constraints, goals, history, and operating context;
- supporting continuity across interactions;
- distinguishing durable context from transient conversation;
- allowing later reasoning to consider prior context;
- maintaining memory as evidence or context rather than final authority.

## Constitutional Responsibilities

Business Memory must:

- serve reasoning rather than replace it;
- remain subject to Authority and Epistemology;
- preserve useful context without inflating certainty;
- distinguish current state from historical state;
- support correction, revision, and supersession;
- avoid becoming an implicit decision engine.

Business Memory must not:

- treat past statements as permanently true;
- commit ungoverned assumptions as durable fact;
- override present evidence without reasoning;
- decide what should happen next;
- define SME Brain's identity through stored data alone.

## Future Evolution

Future memory systems may become richer, more temporal, more structured, and more capable of representing business change.

The constitutional principle remains: memory is durable context for reasoning, not truth itself and not judgment itself.

## Relationship To The Cognitive Runtime

Within the Cognitive Runtime, Business Memory informs situation framing, evidence evaluation, knowledge application, and judgment.

Memory participates throughout cognition as context, but Commit governs when new information may become memory.
