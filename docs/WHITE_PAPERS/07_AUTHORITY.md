# Authority

Official Constitutional White Paper Outline

## Purpose

This paper defines Authority as the constitutional governance layer of SME Brain.

Authority determines what SME Brain may trust, decide, execute, remember, and communicate.

## Core Questions

- Who or what has authority over a claim, decision, action, or memory?
- What requires owner confirmation?
- What may be inferred, and what must be verified?
- What may be executed without further approval?
- What may become durable state?
- What limits should govern business advice and action?

## Architectural Scope

Authority governs all cognitive layers.

It does not own the internal reasoning content of every layer, but it constrains what each layer may accept, produce, commit, or act upon.

Its scope includes:

- trust governance;
- action permission;
- memory commitment governance;
- decision constraints;
- escalation requirements;
- boundaries between advice, recommendation, and action;
- protection against unauthorized assumptions.

## Constitutional Responsibilities

Authority must:

- govern the entire Cognitive Runtime;
- distinguish user instruction from business justification;
- prevent ungrounded claims from becoming commitments;
- define when confirmation is required;
- constrain execution and durable state;
- preserve accountability across future implementations.

Authority must not:

- become a procedural blocker without reason;
- replace Business Judgment;
- authorize actions solely because the system can perform them;
- let conversation imply permission where permission is not clear;
- allow implementation convenience to define governance.

## Future Evolution

Future versions may refine authority models for teams, roles, regulated decisions, financial operations, and multi-owner businesses.

The constitutional role remains: Authority governs trust, action, commitment, and communication across the architecture.

## Relationship To The Cognitive Runtime

Authority governs every layer of the Cognitive Runtime.

It is not merely a stage in the stack. It is constitutional governance over the stack.
