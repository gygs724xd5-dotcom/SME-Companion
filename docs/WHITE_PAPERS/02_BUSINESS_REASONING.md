# Business Reasoning

Official Constitutional White Paper Outline

## Purpose

This paper defines Business Reasoning as the cognitive discipline that interprets the business situation before judgment, decision, execution, or conversation.

Business Reasoning explains how SME Brain moves from user expression and available context toward a structured understanding of what is happening in the business.

## Core Questions

- What business situation is under attention?
- What is the user explicitly asking for?
- What may the user actually need?
- What context is already available?
- What information is missing, uncertain, outdated, or ambiguous?
- What business objectives, constraints, risks, and opportunities matter?
- What reasoning is needed before any action is justified?

## Architectural Scope

Business Reasoning owns interpretation of the situation.

It does not own durable memory, reusable knowledge, final judgment, decision authority, execution, or conversation style.

Its scope includes:

- identifying the business situation under consideration;
- separating the user's words from the underlying business need;
- recognizing relevant business context;
- identifying uncertainty that materially affects reasoning;
- framing alternatives without prematurely choosing among them;
- preparing the situation for Business Judgment.

## Constitutional Responsibilities

Business Reasoning must:

- treat the business situation as primary;
- resist converting every request into a workflow;
- distinguish facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and open questions;
- surface material uncertainty;
- use memory and knowledge as inputs, not authorities;
- prepare reasoning in a form that Judgment can evaluate.

Business Reasoning must not:

- decide merely because a procedure exists;
- ask questions only to satisfy a process;
- treat retrieved context as automatically current;
- convert ambiguity into certainty for convenience;
- collapse reasoning into response generation.

## Future Evolution

Future versions may refine the categories of business situations, improve reasoning over complex multi-party contexts, and support deeper temporal understanding of business change.

These improvements must remain implementation-independent. The constitutional role remains stable: Business Reasoning frames the situation so Judgment can assess it responsibly.

## Relationship To The Cognitive Runtime

Within the Cognitive Runtime, Business Reasoning operates after perception and before Business Judgment.

It receives present input, available context, evidence, epistemic status, ontology, memory, and knowledge. It prepares a coherent business situation for Judgment without deciding the final action.
