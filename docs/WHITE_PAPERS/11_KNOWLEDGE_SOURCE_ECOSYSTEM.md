# Knowledge Source Ecosystem

Official Constitutional White Paper Outline

## Purpose

This paper defines the future knowledge source ecosystem of SME Brain.

It explains how SME Brain should reason across distributed sources of information without attempting to contain all knowledge within itself.

SME Brain should know where knowledge may exist, how knowledge sources should be classified, how source quality should be evaluated, and how information may support reasoning.

It should not treat any source of information as the identity of the Brain.

## Core Doctrine

SME Brain should never attempt to know everything.

Knowledge is distributed.

Reasoning is centralized.

Architecture is permanent.

Knowledge sources evolve.

Models evolve.

SME Brain remains SME Brain.

The constitutional intelligence of SME Brain comes from its business reasoning architecture, not from any individual knowledge provider, search provider, document store, database, or foundation model.

## Constitutional Principle

Knowledge Source is not Business Knowledge.

A Knowledge Source provides information.

Business Knowledge provides business doctrine.

These responsibilities must remain separate.

If a source contains facts, records, documents, regulations, reports, or prior model knowledge, that source may inform Evidence.

It does not automatically become SME doctrine.

Business Knowledge remains the reusable body of business understanding owned by SME Brain. It includes principles, patterns, practices, constraints, and domain reasoning for areas such as pricing, cost, inventory, promotion, cash flow, supplier management, operations, customers, products, and strategy.

Knowledge sources may supply material that Business Knowledge can reason about, but they do not replace constitutional business reasoning.

## Core Questions

- Where may relevant information exist?
- What kind of source is this?
- Is the source internal or external to the business?
- Is the source structured or unstructured?
- What reliability should be expected from this source?
- How fresh or stale is the source likely to be?
- For what business purposes is the source appropriate?
- What constitutional limits apply to use of this source?
- How should Evidence and Truth Status treat information from this source?
- How can SME Brain reason across changing sources while preserving one architecture?

## Architectural Scope

The Knowledge Source Ecosystem defines the constitutional place of information sources in SME Brain.

It does not define retrieval, search, indexing, crawling, connectors, databases, vector stores, document parsing, web integration, API contracts, data pipelines, synchronization, or implementation mechanisms.

Its scope includes:

- recognizing that business-relevant information may live outside SME Brain;
- classifying future sources of evidence and knowledge material;
- preserving the separation between information source, evidence, truth status, business knowledge, judgment, and decision;
- describing source metadata that future runtime layers may rely on;
- protecting model independence and provider independence;
- ensuring that source expansion does not collapse constitutional reasoning boundaries.

## Knowledge Source Categories

Future SME Brain systems may reason with many kinds of information sources.

Internal sources may include:

- Business Memory;
- Store Profile;
- Product Catalog;
- Inventory;
- Sales Database;
- Customer Database;
- Supplier Database;
- OCR Documents;
- Uploaded Files;
- Business Knowledge Packs.

External sources may include:

- Web Search;
- Wikipedia;
- Government Data;
- Industry Reports;
- Accounting Standards;
- Financial Reports;
- Regulations;
- Foundation Model Prior Knowledge.

These categories are architectural examples, not implementation commitments.

The existence of a source category does not imply that SME Brain currently retrieves from it, searches it, connects to it, stores it, or treats it as authoritative.

## Evidence Source Registry

The future Evidence Source Registry should classify knowledge and evidence sources.

The registry is an architectural construct for source awareness.

It is not a retrieval engine.

It is not a search system.

It is not a database.

It is not a web integration layer.

A future registry may describe sources using attributes such as:

- source_name;
- source_category;
- internal_or_external;
- structured_or_unstructured;
- expected_reliability;
- update_frequency;
- intended_usage;
- constitutional_notes.

These attributes define how a source should be understood by the cognitive architecture.

They do not define how information is obtained.

## Future Runtime Relationship

The future runtime relationship is:

```text
Reality

-> Perception

-> Business Situation

-> Evidence Runtime

-> Evidence Source Registry

-> Truth Status

-> Perspective

-> Knowledge

-> Business Judgment

-> Decision
```

This flow describes constitutional dependence, not implementation sequence.

Evidence Runtime may become aware of source classes through the Evidence Source Registry.

Truth Status may depend on source classification when evaluating reliability, freshness, relevance, conflict, and justified reliance.

Knowledge may apply business doctrine after evidence and truth status have been considered.

Business Judgment remains responsible for contextual business assessment.

Decision remains responsible for selecting the next responsible direction.

## Truth Status Relationship

Truth Status evaluates evidence.

Truth Status does not search for evidence.

Truth Status does not retrieve knowledge.

Truth Status does not own source discovery.

Truth Status depends on source awareness because reliability, freshness, provenance, conflict, and intended usage affect justified reliance.

The Evidence Source Registry supports Truth Status by making source characteristics explicit.

Truth Status remains accountable to Evidence. It determines whether claims are justified enough for the current business context.

It must not treat a source as authoritative merely because it is available, familiar, official-sounding, model-generated, or frequently used.

## Business Knowledge Relationship

Business Knowledge is not the Internet.

Business Knowledge is not a search result.

Business Knowledge is not a database.

Business Knowledge is not foundation model memory.

Business Knowledge represents SME doctrine.

It is the reusable business understanding SME Brain applies after situation framing, evidence awareness, truth status, and perspective.

Knowledge sources may provide information about markets, regulations, documents, transactions, products, customers, suppliers, financial statements, or industry practices.

Business Knowledge determines how business doctrine can responsibly interpret that information.

## Constitutional Responsibilities

The Knowledge Source Ecosystem must:

- preserve the distinction between source, evidence, truth, knowledge, judgment, and decision;
- allow SME Brain to reason across many sources without making any source constitutionally central;
- keep source classification separate from source retrieval;
- support Truth Status by making source characteristics explicit;
- protect Business Knowledge as doctrine rather than information storage;
- preserve model independence and provider independence;
- make future source expansion subordinate to the cognitive runtime.

The Knowledge Source Ecosystem must not:

- implement retrieval;
- implement search;
- implement web integration;
- implement databases;
- define connectors, indexes, crawlers, embeddings, sync jobs, or storage schemas;
- treat access to information as equivalent to justified truth;
- treat Business Knowledge Packs as the same thing as general source material;
- allow foundation model prior knowledge to bypass Evidence or Truth Status;
- let external providers redefine SME Brain's constitutional reasoning process.

## Model And Provider Independence

Knowledge sources may change.

Foundation models may change.

Search providers may change.

Internal systems may change.

External data providers may change.

The constitutional reasoning process must remain unchanged.

SME Brain may use different sources over time, but it must still reason through the same responsibility boundaries.

The identity of SME Brain comes from constitutional business reasoning rather than any individual knowledge provider.

## Future Evolution

Future versions of SME Brain may become capable of reasoning across richer internal records, uploaded documents, external references, regulatory material, industry data, and model-provided prior knowledge.

That evolution should expand the information available to Evidence and Truth Status.

It should not move reasoning ownership into the source layer.

The long-term vision is a Brain that can discover, evaluate, integrate, and reason over distributed knowledge while preserving one constitutional business reasoning process.

The architecture owns reasoning.

Knowledge sources provide information.

## Relationship To The Cognitive Runtime

Within the Cognitive Runtime, knowledge sources sit upstream of justified reliance and downstream of reality-facing observation.

They inform Evidence.

They support Truth Status through source characteristics.

They may supply material considered by Knowledge.

They do not own Judgment, Decision, Authority, Conversation, Execution, or Commit.

The Knowledge Source Ecosystem extends the architecture's source awareness while preserving the central runtime doctrine:

information is not truth;

truth is not judgment;

knowledge sources are not Business Knowledge;

distributed knowledge must remain subordinate to centralized reasoning.
