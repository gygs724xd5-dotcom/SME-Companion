# SME Brain

Official Constitutional White Paper Outline

## Purpose

This paper defines SME Brain as the integrated Business Cognitive Architecture formed by the complete white paper collection.

It explains the identity of the whole system rather than any single layer.

## Core Questions

- What is SME Brain as a complete cognitive architecture?
- How do the constitutional papers relate to each other?
- What makes SME Brain different from a chatbot, workflow system, automation platform, or model wrapper?
- What must remain stable when applications and models change?
- How should future work preserve the identity of SME Brain?

## Architectural Scope

This paper owns the integrated identity of SME Brain.

It does not duplicate the specific responsibilities of Cognitive Runtime, Business Reasoning, Memory, Knowledge, Judgment, Decision, Authority, Epistemology, or Ontology.

Its scope includes:

- defining the complete Business Cognitive Architecture;
- explaining how the constitutional papers form one system;
- preserving the distinction between identity and implementation;
- guiding future applications, products, and technical systems back to the constitutional center.

## Constitutional Responsibilities

SME Brain as a whole must:

- improve the owner's business situation through contextual reasoning;
- preserve cognitive responsibility boundaries;
- keep Judgment at the center;
- keep Authority over trust, action, and commitment;
- use Memory and Knowledge as inputs to reasoning;
- use Decision to select responsible direction;
- use Execution only as a subordinate capability;
- use Conversation to communicate help clearly;
- remain model-independent and implementation-independent.

SME Brain as a whole must not:

- become a workflow engine with conversational language;
- become a foundation model wrapper with project branding;
- define itself through source code or infrastructure;
- let applications redefine constitutional identity;
- let useful behavior override architecture when the two conflict.

## Future Evolution

SME Brain may evolve into many products, interfaces, deployments, and model executions.

Future evolution should expand capability without changing constitutional identity.

The proper test is not whether future systems resemble today's implementation. The proper test is whether they preserve the same cognitive responsibilities and reasoning doctrine.

## Relationship To The Cognitive Runtime

The Cognitive Runtime is the operational ordering of SME Brain's reasoning.

This paper defines the integrated identity of the Brain that the runtime serves. The runtime explains how the Brain thinks; this paper explains what the Brain is.
