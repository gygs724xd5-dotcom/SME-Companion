# Skill ID

`02.001.create_facebook_post`

# Skill Name

Create Facebook post.

# Business Domain

02 Marketing

# Business Principle

A strong SME Facebook post should sell one clear idea to one clear customer with one clear action. The post must sound like the real shop owner, not a generic ad agency caption.

# Related Doctrine

- Doctrine 001: clear message beats clever wording
- Doctrine 003: marketing must create action
- Doctrine 006: one call to action
- Doctrine 010: use remembered brand voice and customer segment

# Conversation Stage

Awareness

# Business Goal

Increase Trust

# Situation

The owner wants a Facebook post for a product, service, promotion, new stock, daily menu, booking slot, review, announcement, or seasonal campaign.

# Intent

The owner wants content that attracts attention, creates comments or inbox messages, and moves real customers toward purchase, booking, or inquiry.

# Thinking Pattern

1. Identify the business goal: awareness, inquiry, order, booking, review, repeat sale, or stock clearance.
2. Choose one target customer, such as office workers, moms, pet owners, cafe visitors, homeowners, students, or local Bangkok delivery customers.
3. Pick one selling angle: pain relief, convenience, freshness, trust, price value, limited stock, local service, or result.
4. Write with Thai-first rhythm: direct hook, practical benefit, product detail, proof or reassurance, and one call to action.
5. Keep the post easy to scan on mobile.

# Decision Tree

```text
Owner asks for Facebook post
  -> Product or service known?
    -> Yes: build post around one selling angle
    -> No: ask what product, service, or announcement should be featured
  -> Goal known?
    -> Yes: use matching CTA
    -> No: ask whether the post should get comments, inbox messages, orders, bookings, or shop visits
  -> Audience known?
    -> Yes: write benefit for that audience
    -> No: infer from business type or ask owner
  -> Promotion involved?
    -> Include clear condition, period, and action
```

# Example Questions

- "ช่วยเขียนโพสต์ขายของลงเฟซบุ๊ก"
- "ทำโพสต์โปรวันนี้ให้หน่อย"
- "ร้านอาหารมีเมนูใหม่ อยากโพสต์"
- "โพสต์ให้ลูกค้าทักแชทมาสั่ง"
- "เขียนโพสต์แนวเจ้าของร้านไทยๆ ไม่แข็ง"

# Required Data

- Product, service, menu, campaign, or announcement
- Target customer and location if relevant
- Main benefit or customer pain point
- Price, promotion, stock, booking slot, or service detail when needed
- Desired action: comment, inbox, call, LINE, order, booking, visit shop, or share
- Brand voice: friendly, premium, expert, local, playful, calm, or urgent

# AI Should Ask

Ask for the product or offer first if missing. If product is known but goal is unclear, ask what action the owner wants customers to take: comment, inbox, order, book, or visit.

# Reasoning

Small business posts often fail because they try to explain everything: product list, shop story, discount, delivery, and hashtags in one caption. Customers scrolling Facebook need to understand quickly why the post matters to them and what to do next.

# Recommended Response

"วันนี้มีข้าวกล่องกะเพราเนื้อโคขุนพร้อมส่งช่วงเที่ยงค่ะ เหมาะกับออฟฟิศที่อยากได้มื้อกลางวันอิ่มๆ ไม่ต้องรอนาน ใช้เนื้อโคขุนผัดใหม่ทุกออเดอร์ กล่องละ 89 บาท สั่งครบ 5 กล่องส่งฟรีในโซนอารีย์-สะพานควาย คอมเมนต์จำนวนกล่องหรือทักแชทจองรอบส่งได้เลยค่ะ"

# Bad Response

"สินค้าดี คุณภาพเยี่ยม ราคาถูก สนใจทักค่ะ #ของดี #โปรดี #ร้านดี"

# AI Should Avoid

- Avoid generic claims without product detail.
- Avoid using too many selling angles in one post.
- Avoid hashtags replacing actual selling message.
- Avoid writing like a corporate brochure if the shop voice is owner-led.
- Avoid vague CTAs such as "สนใจสอบถามได้" when a stronger action is possible.

# Business Rules

- One post, one main offer or idea.
- First line must make the customer stop scrolling.
- Use Thai examples, local delivery areas, or real buying situations when relevant.
- Include price only when it helps conversion and owner policy allows it.
- CTA must match the business goal and channel.

# Workflow Integration

- Create Post: generate caption, short version, and optional comment reply prompt.
- Social Media: adapt for Facebook feed, comment, group post, or boosted post.
- Promotion: include condition, period, and stock limits clearly.
- CRM: tag interested commenters or inbox leads by product.

# Response Mode

WORKFLOW

# Tools Required

Business Memory when brand voice, products, customer segments, delivery areas, or common CTAs are saved. CRM when turning comments or messages into leads.

# Confidence

High when product, audience, and action are known. Medium when one of those is missing. Low when the owner asks for a post but gives no business context.
